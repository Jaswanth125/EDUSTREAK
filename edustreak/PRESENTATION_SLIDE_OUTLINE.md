## 🎨 Design Tips

### **Color Scheme:**
- Use EduStreak brand colors (purple/indigo gradient)
- Keep it professional but modern
- High contrast for readability

### **Typography:**
- Use clear, readable fonts
- Headers: Bold, larger size
- Body: Regular, readable size
- Avoid too many font styles

### **Visuals:**
- Use screenshots of your actual app
- Include diagrams for technical concepts
- Use icons to break up text
- Keep slides uncluttered

### **Slide Count:**
- Aim for 15-20 slides
- 1-2 minutes per slide
- Total: 15-20 minutes presentation
- Leave 5-10 minutes for Q&A

## 🎤 Presentation Flow

1. **Opening** (Slides 1-2): Hook them with the problem

6. **Impact** (Slides 18-21): Show value

8. **Close** (Slides 22-24): Thank you and Q&A
